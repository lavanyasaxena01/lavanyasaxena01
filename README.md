## 👋 About Me

I'm **Lavanya Saxena**, a B.Tech undergraduate in **Artificial
Intelligence & Data Science** at Dr. Akhilesh Das Gupta Institute of
Professional Studies, affiliated with GGSIPU, Delhi.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical, data-driven applications---from
predictive analytics and machine learning to computer vision and
geospatial AI. I'm especially interested in building AI systems that are
useful, explainable, and grounded in real-world problems.

-   🔭 Building and experimenting with AI/ML applications
-   🛰️ Exploring satellite imagery, change detection, and multimodal
    geospatial AI
-   📊 Interested in predictive modeling, data analytics, and
    forecasting
-   🔬 Exploring explainable AI and medical image analysis
-   🤝 Open to collaborating on meaningful AI, data science, and
    open-source projects
